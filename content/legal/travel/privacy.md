# Atlas AI Travel Planner — Privacy Policy

## 1. Who we are and what this policy covers
This policy covers the **Atlas AI Travel Planner**: the website at getatlas.ca/travel and travel.getatlas.ca, our travel blog and community pages, and the **Atlas** Android app (`ca.getatlas.app`). In this policy, "the Planner" means all of these.

The Planner is provided by **{{legalName}}**, a sole proprietor in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, operating under the registered business name **{{tradeName}}** (called "{{brandName}}" on this website; "**we**", "**us**").

If you contacted us about our business technology services, see the separate [Technology Services Privacy Notice](/legal/services/privacy).

## 2. Summary
- We collect your sign-in details, the messages you send to the Planner, the trips you save, and the settings you choose.
- **Your messages are sent to AI providers in the United States** (Groq, Google Gemini and Anthropic) so they can write the answers.
- If you allow location, we use it **only to work out your city**.
- We use **Google Analytics** to understand how the website is used.
- **Shared trips are public.** Anyone with the link can see them, and they can appear in search engines.
- You can delete your account at any time, in the Android app or at [getatlas.ca/account/delete](/account/delete).
- We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

## 4. How we use your information
- To provide the Planner: answer your questions, build trip plans and keep your trips and conversations.
- To run your account and subscription, including billing and support.
- To apply usage limits, keep the Planner secure and prevent misuse.
- To send emails you asked for (subscription confirmation, newsletter) and replies to your messages.
- To understand how the Planner is used and improve it.
- To meet our legal obligations, such as tax record-keeping.

If we want to use your information for a new purpose, we will ask you first.

## 5. AI providers
To answer you, the Planner sends your message, the earlier messages in that conversation and your personalisation (such as your city and passport country) to one of these AI providers:

- **Groq, Inc.** (Llama models)
- **Google LLC** (Gemini API)
- **Anthropic, PBC** (Claude models)

We do not add your name or email address to these requests. Please do not type information you don't want to share, such as passport numbers or health details.

How each provider handles your messages, under its own terms:
- **Groq** does not keep your messages by default. It may keep them for up to 30 days to investigate errors or abuse.
- **Anthropic** does not use them to train its models, and keeps them only for a limited time.
- **Google** keeps them for a limited time to prevent misuse. When Google's free Gemini API service is used, Google may also use messages and answers to improve its products, and people at Google may read them.

AI answers can be wrong. See section 4 of our [Terms of Service](/terms).

## 6. Location
- **Website:** if you allow location, your browser sends your coordinates directly to **OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service** to look up your city. We keep only "City, Country" and add it to your messages.
- **Android app:** the app asks for location permission when you first open the home screen. If you allow it, the app looks up your city on your device, saves the city on your device and adds it to your messages.
- Location is optional. You can turn it off at any time in your browser or phone settings, and the Planner still works without it.

## 7. Google Calendar
If you choose **Add to Google Calendar**, we ask Google for permission to **create events in your calendar** (permission `calendar.events`). We use it only to add the trip events you choose. We don't read, store or share your other calendar information, and we don't use Google data for advertising or to train AI models. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the **Google API Services User Data Policy**, including its Limited Use requirements. You can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## 8. Cookies, browser storage and analytics
- **Essential storage:** the website stores your sign-in session, settings and your current conversation in your browser's storage. The current conversation is not reloaded after 24 hours and is replaced by your next one. The Android app stores the same kind of information on your phone.
- **Google Analytics:** the website uses Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC, United States). It sets cookies and collects pages viewed, device and browser information, approximate location based on your IP address, and sign-up and sign-in events. You can block it with Google's opt-out browser add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) or by blocking cookies in your browser.
- **Travel articles:** some blog pages show a **GetYourGuide** activity widget, which may set its own cookies. Some links on those pages are affiliate links, so we may earn a commission if you book.
- We don't use advertising networks and we don't sell your personal information.

## 9. Shared trips and community posts
- **Shared trips:** when you share a trip, anyone with the link can see the trip and its conversation. Shared trips are listed for search engines, so they can appear in search results. Don't share trips that contain personal details. To stop sharing, make the trip private or delete it. Search engines may keep copies for a while after that.
- **Community stories and comments** are public once published.

## 11. Where your information is processed
Most of our providers are in the **United States**, and some operate in other countries or on global networks. Your information may therefore be stored and processed outside Canada. While it is there, the laws of that country apply, and its courts, law enforcement and national security authorities may be able to access it.

## 12. How long we keep it
| Information | How long |
|---|---|
| Account, saved trips, conversations and other content stored with us | While your account is open. You can delete trips at any time |
| Current conversation in your browser | Not reloaded after 24 hours; replaced by your next conversation; removed when you clear your browser data |
| Shared trips | Until you stop sharing or delete the trip |
| Payment records (receipts and invoices) | Six years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as Canadian tax law requires. Stripe and Google Play hold these records |
| Newsletter | Until you unsubscribe. We then keep your email marked as unsubscribed so we don't email you again |
| Backups | Deleted when the backups are overwritten |

**When you delete your account**, we delete your sign-in account and profile, saved and shared trips, trip projects, conversations and messages, usage counts, notification sign-ups, community stories, the subscription records we hold, any free-access entry, and the newsletter subscription for the same email address.

We do not automatically delete: comments you posted on blog articles (email us and we will remove them); records of AI usage costs, which keep a random account number but not your name or email; and payment records held by Stripe or Google Play.

## 13. Your choices and rights
- **See or correct your information:** email us. We reply within 30 days.
- **Delete your account:** in the Android app (version 1.3.0 or later), go to **Settings → Delete account**. On the website, or with any app version, go to [getatlas.ca/account/delete](/account/delete) and sign in with the same Google account. If you have an active website subscription, you will be asked to cancel it first. A Google Play subscription is not cancelled automatically, so cancel it in Google Play. If you signed in with an email link, or prefer email, write to support@getatlas.ca from your account email with the subject "Delete my Atlas account".
- **Withdraw consent:** you can turn off location, notifications or personalisation, unsubscribe from the newsletter, or block analytics at any time. Some features may then stop working.
- **Complain:** contact us first. You can also complain to the **Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada** (priv.gc.ca).

## 14. Age limit
The Planner is for people aged **18** and over. If you believe someone younger has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 15. Security
We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), our providers' encryption of stored data, access controls and limits on who can see your information. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will tell you and report it to the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, as the law requires.
