# Technology Services Privacy Notice

## 1. Who we are and what this notice covers
**{{legalName}}**, a sole proprietor in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, operating under the registered business name **{{tradeName}}** (called "{{brandName}}" on this website; "**we**", "**us**"). We build automations, CRMs, websites and AI assistants for businesses.

This notice covers personal information about:
- people who contact us, or ask for a free audit, a free setup, a proposal or a quote;
- our clients' owners, staff and other contacts; and
- other business contacts.

**Our clients' customers.** When we build or run a system for a client, such as a missed-call text-back or a follow-up system, we handle the client's customers' information **on the client's behalf**, as its service provider. The client decides how that information is used, and the client's privacy policy applies to it. If you are a customer of one of our clients, please contact that business first.

The **Atlas AI Travel Planner** has its own [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## 2. Who is responsible
Our Privacy Officer, **{{privacyOfficer}}**, is responsible for how we handle personal information. Contact details are in section 14.

## 3. What we collect
| Information | Examples | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Enquiry details** | Name, business email, company, phone or WhatsApp number, the service you're interested in, your current tools, timeline, budget range and message | You, through our contact form or by email |
| **Consent record** | That you ticked the consent box, when, which version of the wording, and a reference number for your request | Our website |
| **Where you came from** | Campaign tags in the link you used, and the website and page that sent you to us (not the full address) | Our website |
| **Conversations** | Emails, messages, call notes, proposals, quotes and contracts | You and us |
| **Free audit results** | When and how your business replied to the test enquiry you asked us to send | Our test of your public contact channels |
| **Client records** | Billing contact, invoices, payment status, project notes and approvals | You and our records |
| **Access details** | Logins or keys you give us so we can build your system | You |

Our business services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children through them.

## 4. How we use it
- To reply to you, run the free audit or free setup you asked for, and prepare proposals and quotes.
- To deliver, support and invoice the services you buy.
- To keep records of consent, approvals, contracts and payments.
- To see which pages and campaigns bring us enquiries, using only the "where you came from" information above.
- To protect our systems and prevent misuse.
- To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We use your information only for these purposes, or for purposes a reasonable person would expect. If we want to use it for something new, we will ask you first.

## 5. How we use AI tools
We use AI tools, provided by AI companies mainly in the United States, to help us summarise enquiries and draft emails and proposals. We don't use your information to train AI models, and we don't make important decisions about you using AI alone.

## 6. Marketing emails
We send marketing emails only if you agreed, or if Canada's anti-spam law otherwise allows it, for example when you are already a client. Every marketing email names us, includes our mailing address and has an unsubscribe link. We act on unsubscribe requests within 10 business days.

## 7. Who receives your information
We don't sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, and only as much as they need:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Vercel and Cloudflare | Run, deliver and protect our website and contact form |
| n8n, running on a server we rent | Checks new enquiries and sends them to our CRM |
| Supabase | Database for our CRM: enquiries, follow-up tasks and activity records |
| Discord | Sends us an alert when a new enquiry arrives |
| Zoho Mail | Our business email |
| Google Fonts | Delivers the fonts on our website. Google receives your IP address and browser details |
| AI service providers | Summarising and drafting (section 5) |
| Payment providers | Processing your payments, for example your bank or card processor |
| Other business software | Email, calendar, documents, accounting and similar tools we use to manage our work and client relationships |
| Lawyers, accountants and insurers | Professional advice, under confidentiality |

People who work for us or on our behalf see client and enquiry information only when they need it for their work, and under written confidentiality obligations.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety or our legal rights, or if the business is sold or reorganised. In a sale or reorganisation, your information stays protected under this notice.

## 8. Where your information is processed
Many of our providers are in the **United States**, and some operate in other countries or on global networks. Your information may therefore be stored and processed outside Canada. While it is there, the laws of that country apply, and its courts, law enforcement and national security authorities may be able to access it.

## 9. How long we keep it
| Information | How long |
|---|---|
| Enquiries that don't become a client relationship | Up to **24 months** after our last contact, unless the law or a dispute requires longer, or you ask us to delete it sooner |
| Client records, contracts and invoices | For the relationship, then **six years** after the end of the tax year they relate to, as Canadian tax law requires |
| Consent and unsubscribe records | As long as we need them to prove consent or respect your choice |
| Access details you give us | We remove our access when the work ends |
| Our clients' customers' information | As the client instructs and our contract with the client says |

## 10. How we protect it
- Encrypted connections (HTTPS) for our website and forms.
- Access limited to the people who need it.
- Secret keys kept out of our website code.
- Our contact form's server logs do not record the content of your enquiry.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will tell you and report it to the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, as the law requires. We keep a record of every breach.

## 11. Your rights
You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask how we used it and who we shared it with, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent (subject to legal or contract limits). Contact our Privacy Officer. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the **Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada** (priv.gc.ca).

## 12. Cookies on our business pages
Our business pages (getatlas.ca, /services, /pricing, /faq, /contact and /for/…) don't use advertising or analytics cookies. They load fonts from Google Fonts (section 7). Our network provider, Cloudflare, may set a strictly necessary security cookie.

## 13. Changes to this notice
We will post any new version here with its effective date. If we want to use your information for a new purpose, we will ask for your consent first.
