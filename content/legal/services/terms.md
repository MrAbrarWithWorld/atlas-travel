### 30-day money-back guarantee on the setup fee
If you are not happy with your setup, tell us in writing within **30 days after your system goes live**. We will refund the **setup fee** you paid for that plan. You don't have to give a reason.
- The guarantee covers the setup fee only. Monthly fees for the time your system was running, and third-party costs already paid, are not refunded.
- After a guarantee refund, we switch off what we built and return or delete your data.
- The guarantee applies once per client for each plan. If your setup was free, there is no setup fee to refund, and you can cancel the monthly plan at any time.

### Monthly plans: cancel anytime
- Monthly plans have no long-term contract unless your statement of work says otherwise.
- To cancel, email support@getatlas.ca. Cancellation takes effect at the **end of the month you have already paid for**, and we don't charge you after that.
- We don't refund part of a month, except for any period in which we didn't provide the service. For that period we refund or credit you.

### Project services
- If you cancel a project before it is finished, we refund any payment for stages of work **not yet started**. We keep payment for work already done and for third-party costs already paid, and we give you the work completed so far.

### Third-party costs
Some systems need paid third-party services, such as phone numbers and text messages, AI usage, a server, domains or software subscriptions. We list them in your quote. Unless your quote says otherwise, you pay them directly or we pass them on **at cost**. Third parties generally don't refund these costs, so we can't either once they are paid.
