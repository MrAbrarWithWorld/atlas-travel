Delete your Atlas account

You can permanently delete your Atlas account (website and Android app) and the data connected to it at any time.

Delete it here

Sign in with the same Google account you use for Atlas, then confirm.

Not you? Sign out

In the Android app

  1. Open Atlas and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

Email support@getatlas.ca from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my Atlas account”. Requests are completed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Saved trips and trip projects, including shared trip links
  • Chat history, preferences and push-notification settings
  • Community stories you submitted

What may be kept

  • Payment and invoice records that the law requires us to keep (for example invoices held by Stripe or Google Play)
  • Anonymous usage statistics that no longer identify you

Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel a Google Play subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. If you have an active website (Stripe) subscription, cancel it first or email us and we will cancel it for you.
